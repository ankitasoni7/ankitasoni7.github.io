Hi,

We've observed that many people begin therapy but discontinue after just one or two sessions, while others never seek professional help despite needing support. We believe barriers such as accessibility, time constraints, and stigma may be contributing to this, but we don't yet understand the root causes.

### What We Aim to Understand

We're looking to explore:

* Why people hesitate to seek or continue mental healthcare.
* The biggest challenges they face throughout their care journey.
* Whether a digital solution could meaningfully improve access and continuity of care.

At this stage, we're not certain whether an app is the right approach. We're looking for research-backed insights and recommendations before deciding on the best solution.
